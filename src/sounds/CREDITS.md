# Sound credits

All animal voices are real recordings, trimmed, cleaned up and level-matched for Desktop Pal.

| Animal | Files | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cat | cat-pet (purr), cat-happy, cat-happy2, cat-call | BigSoundBank, Joseph Sardin: "Cat purr" (#0436), "Small mewing of a cat" (#0098), "Little meow of a cat #1" (#0494), "Meow cat #1" (#1889) | CC0 (public domain) |
| Dog | dog-pet, dog-happy, dog-call, dog-call2 | BigSoundBank: "Barking Dog #2" (#2954) | CC0 (public domain) |
| Fox | fox-pet, fox-happy, fox-call | U.S. National Park Service / Shan Burson, Yellowstone National Park: "Red Fox" | Public domain (U.S. government work) |
| Otter | otter-pet, otter-happy, otter-call | U.S. National Park Service / Canyonlands National Park: "River otters" | Public domain (U.S. government work) |

The red panda's voice (soft twittering and a "huff-quack") is synthesized in code, modelled on the calls
described in Cao et al., "Vocal repertoire of adult captive red pandas (Ailurus fulgens)", 2016.
