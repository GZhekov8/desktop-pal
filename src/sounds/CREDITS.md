The tiger's friendly chuffs (when petted or happy) are synthesized in code from how real chuffs sound:
short, fluttering puffs of air through the nose.

The red panda's voice (soft twittering and a "huff-quack") is synthesized in code, modelled on the calls
described in Cao et al., "Vocal repertoire of adult captive red pandas (Ailurus fulgens)", 2016.
