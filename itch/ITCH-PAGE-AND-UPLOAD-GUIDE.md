## 3. Description (copy and paste into the "Description" box)

---

**Desktop Pal** is a little animal that lives in the corner of your screen and reminds you to do things: drink water, take a break, pay that bill, call your mom.

When something is due, your pal wakes up, waves, makes a face that matches the reminder and shows a speech bubble. Click **Done** or **Snooze**, and it goes back to relaxing.

**Pick your pal**
- 🦦 Sea otter floating on its back
- 🐱 Ginger cat on a cushion
- 🐶 Dog in its bed
- 🦊 Red fox curled up on moss
- 🐾 Red panda lounging on a branch

Every pet is hand-crafted with thousands of individual fur strands, and you can give yours any name you like.

**Reminders that fit your day**
- **Once:** "Dentist at 14:30 on Friday"
- **Daily / weekly:** "Team stand-up every weekday at 10:00"
- **Every X minutes:** "Drink water every hour between 9:00 and 19:00" (overnight windows work too)
- **To-do list:** your pal nudges you about open to-dos every so often

**Nine facial expressions.** Your pal picks a face to match each reminder: serious for bills and deadlines, happy for coffee and lunch, surprised for meetings, loving for family, worried when something is overdue, sleepy late at night, and proud when you click Done. You can also choose the face yourself for any reminder.

**Stays out of your way**
- Clicks pass straight through the empty space around your pet
- Drag it anywhere on your screen and it remembers the spot
- Hide it for 30 minutes, an hour or until you bring it back
- Optional chime and Windows notifications
- Can start with Windows

**Privacy.** Everything stays on your computer. No account, no internet needed, no data collected.

**Install**
1. Download the **Installer**, double-click it, and your pal appears in the bottom-right corner.
2. Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the app is new and not code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Click your pal to see what's coming up. Click **Open app** to add reminders and pick your animal.

Prefer not to install? Download the **Portable** zip, unzip it anywhere and run `Desktop Pal.exe`.

**Uninstall:** Windows Settings → Apps → Desktop Pal → Uninstall.

*Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit). Free and open (MIT License). Built with Electron.*
